Topic: Anti-firework statement

Dear Humans,

We are [Armstrnong & Platon], your feline companions who love the comfort and safety of our home. But during this time of year, something happens that shakes our whiskers and makes our tails puff up in fear — fireworks.

We don’t understand those loud bangs and bright flashes. To us, they sound like danger. They make us feel scared, confused, and helpless. We hide under furniture, trembling, unable to enjoy the quiet life we adore.

Please think of all the animals, not just us cats, but dogs, birds, and even the wildlife outside, who suffer from these noisy celebrations. Fireworks may last only moments for you, but for us, the fear lingers.

With love (and hopeful purrs),

Later,
Armstrong & Platon
